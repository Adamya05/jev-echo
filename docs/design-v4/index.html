Method

Copy Jev. Then follow Echo into its mistakes.

Echo’s training target is Jev’s move distribution. When Echo starts driving, Jev labels the unfamiliar states Echo actually reaches. Echo retrains twice.

01

Label

Ask Jev for its full move distribution on raw Snake boards.

02

Distill

Train a 22,212-parameter convolutional network to match those distributions.

03

Recover

Let Echo drive. Ask Jev what it would do wherever Echo ends up. Retrain.
